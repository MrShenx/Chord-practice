How to play: Read the chord to build. Press Listen to Target to hear it, then click the correct number of keys on the panel below to build that chord by ear (or by working it out) — 3 keys for a triad, 4 for an extended (7th) chord. Black keys have a sharp and a flat button stacked together — pick whichever one correctly spells the chord you're building. Press Test My Chord to check — get it right and your knight lands a hit on the foe; get it wrong and the foe hits back.
